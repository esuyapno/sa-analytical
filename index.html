Ekci Suyapno
Founder & Principal
FacilitationAnalyticsStrategy

Ekci Suyapno founded SA Analytical to close a gap she saw repeatedly over fifteen years in international development: the distance between what the evidence says and what organizations are able to agree on. In complex humanitarian and development contexts across Myanmar and the Middle East, she coordinated joint multi-sector assessments, trained the teams who carried them out, led the analysis, and then brought the findings back to the table to facilitate multi-stakeholder strategy. She worked with government bodies, UN agencies, international organizations, and local communities, often in frontline environments where decisions couldn't wait. Few people have been on both sides of that process: building the evidence and getting a room full of competing mandates to act on it. Through SA Analytical, she brings that full cycle to foundation boards and executive teams, designing data-backed decision processes that produce multi-stakeholder alignment and a defensible strategy leaders can stand behind. Ekci holds an M.A. in International Development from the University of Manchester.
