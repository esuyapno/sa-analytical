Who processes data for us

We use a small number of trusted service providers, who process data only on our instructions:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): our email platform for toolkit sign-ups, Health Check results and newsletters.
  • Cloudflare (United States): hosts this website and processes the Health Check request that sends your results. Cloudflare may log technical data such as your IP address for security.
  • Web3Forms: forwards messages from our contact form to our inbox.
  • Google (United States): provides our email inbox and the web fonts on this site. Loading the fonts sends your IP address to Google.

Some of these providers may process data outside Switzerland and the EU. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under Swiss and EU law, such as the Swiss–US and EU–US Data Privacy Frameworks or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Health Check results and messages to us: for as long as needed to follow up with you, and no longer than two years after our last contact, unless we start working together.

Cookies

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site from bots, and the toolkit sign-up form may use one to work correctly.
